**My ranking: 3 > 9 > 1 > 4 > 6 > 2 > 8 > 5 > 7.** Start with the ADA audit’s measurement test; keep the verified web gate as backup.

This ranks relative grand-prize prospects, not measured probabilities. **Read** below means opened product pages, papers, repositories, app listings or government records. **Assessment** means my reasoning. Vendor and hackathon claims remain untested. “Disqualified” means under your established-product constraint, not an organizer ruling.

1. **Walk-through ADA audit — best combination of spectacle and a defensible narrow scope**

   **Read:** [RASSAR](https://makeabilitylab.cs.washington.edu/projects/rassar/) is an almost-exact **research prototype**: phone LiDAR, computer vision, accessibility findings and AR localization. [BlueDAG](https://www.bluedag.com/software.html) is **established** inspection software with regional standards and mobile findings. [AccessScan](https://devpost.com/software/accessscan) is a **hackathon prototype** producing photo-linked accessibility reports. BlueDAG does **not disqualify the automatic scanning version**; RASSAR severely weakens originality.

   **Breaker:** A reconstructed doorway opening is not necessarily usable clear passage. Occlusion, door position and measurement uncertainty can reverse a threshold decision. A [municipal BlueDAG workflow](https://www.indiantrail.org/DocumentCenter/View/4534/May-23-2025-Presentation-to-Council) still uses tape and a level.

   **48-hour assessment:** Conditional yes: actual geometry, three selected checks, pinned citations and explicit uncertainty. Comprehensive compliance and lawsuit protection are shell.

   **Reaction:** “That’s this room”—then move an obstruction and rescan.

   **Judge:** “Does this protect against Unruh claims?” Honest answer: no; preliminary triage.

   **Friday kill:** Scan ten openings/heights three times against tape. Kill automatic verdicts if error exceeds the compliance margin.

2. **Non-visual web agent — viable only around the verification mechanism**

   **Read:** [Guide](https://www.guideinteraction.com/) is a **live commercial product** helping blind users operate inaccessible websites. [Morae](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.21456v1), **UIST research**, pauses agents for user choices. [AccessPilot](https://devpost.com/software/access-pilot), a **hackathon project**, already claims voice interaction, form completion, approval gates, stale-observation rejection and outcome verification. **Generic conversational browsing is disqualified; the exact deterministic gate remains potentially distinct.**

   **Breaker:** DOM values need not equal submitted data. JavaScript, hidden fields, frames and server state defeat simplistic readback. A mutation observer alone is insufficient.

   **48-hour assessment:** Yes for supported forms: real field extraction, approval bound to reviewed values, invalidation and verified outcome. Arbitrary-site safety is shell.

   **Reaction:** Approve $10; the page changes to $1,000; execution refuses.

   **Judge:** “What differs from AccessPilot?” Honest answer requires showing the enforced value-binding mechanism.

   **Friday kill:** Mutate values, checkbox properties, hidden amounts and submit handlers after approval. One unauthorized changed submission kills the safety claim.

3. **Silent voice — emotional upside, recognition risk**

   **Read:** [SRAVI](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10332046/) is a **legacy clinical commercial app**; its maker is [in liquidation](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/NI635074), so current service availability is uncertain. [Tepy](https://tepy.ai/pioneerprogram) is a **pioneer-stage headset** combining personalized lip recognition and voice sampling. [Silencio](https://devpost.com/software/silencio-xoy8pb) is a **hackathon phone lip-to-speech project**. No verified current established exact rival conclusively disqualifies it.

   **Breaker:** Visually similar utterances remain ambiguous. An [oral tube can obscure lips](https://www.ijke.org/vol2/68-A015.pdf); tracheostomy is a different situation. [LipLearner](https://arxiv.org/html/2302.05907v3) achieved 0.895 one-shot F1 for 25 personalized commands—not unrestricted patient conversation.

   **48-hour assessment:** Conditional yes: one calibrated speaker, 10–20 phrases, visible confirmation, consented cloned voice. Open conversation and clinical reliability are shell.

   **Reaction:** A silently mouthed request emerges in the person’s familiar voice.

   **Judge:** “Did context invent that?” Honest answer: show candidates, uncertainty and confirmation.

   **Friday kill:** Randomize 50 unseen trials, including confusing phrases and out-of-vocabulary requests. Confident wrong speech kills it.

4. **Blindfold guide — strongest stunt, weak originality**

   **Read:** [Clew Maps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/clew-maps/id1595185167) is a **small research-origin released app** recording and sharing indoor routes with printable anchors. [Phone-in-pocket backtracking](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08021v1) is **research** requiring no prior map. [GoodMaps](https://www.goodmaps.com/) is **established indoor navigation**, though its page does not establish volunteer single-walk creation. No exact established-service disqualification demonstrated; Clew already covers much of the premise.

   **Breaker:** Recording a trajectory does not solve later relocalization or detect newly moved obstacles.

   **48-hour assessment:** Conditional yes for one short route with a reliable starting anchor. Building-wide navigation and independent travel safety are shell.

   **Reaction:** The judge reaches a specific chair using voice/haptics.

   **Judge:** “What if someone moves a chair?” Honest answer: route guidance does not guarantee obstacle avoidance; use a spotter.

   **Friday kill:** Restart the app, change lighting and phone orientation, then repeat ten routes. Wrong turns or physical interventions kill the blindfold demonstration.

5. **Life-event government agent — useful, but “every obligation” collapses**

   **Read:** [Empathy](https://www.empathy.com/platform/bereavement-care) is **established** personalized administrative support. [Tell Us Once](https://www.gov.uk/after-a-death/organisations-you-need-to-contact-and-tell-us-once) is an **established UK government** death-notification service. [ONCE](https://devpost.com/software/once-h2pdtk) is a close **hackathon prototype**, explicitly coordinating fictional service simulations. These do not establish an exact California equivalent, but parallel-agent animation adds little novelty.

   **Breaker:** One sentence lacks eligibility, jurisdiction and authority information. [SSA says survivor benefits cannot be applied for online](https://www.ssa.gov/faqs/en/questions/KA-02083.html).

   **48-hour assessment:** Yes for one event, one jurisdiction, targeted follow-up questions and two real form drafts. Exhaustive discovery and universal completion are shell.

   **Reaction:** One account becomes several correctly populated official documents.

   **Judge:** “How do you know nothing was missed?” Honest answer: you don’t; publish bounded coverage.

   **Friday kill:** Manually establish the correct agency checklist, then test three materially different households. A missed major obligation kills the universal promise.

6. **Fire claim from memories — easy demo, crowded core**

   **Read:** [AfterProof](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/afterproof-home-inventory/id6761285323) is a **small released app** explicitly rebuilding inventories from old photos. [Yembo](https://yembo.ai/insurance/contents) is **established**, with room-grouped inventories, valuations and photo markers. [Contensure](https://www.contensure.com/) is **live commercial software**, scale unverified, identifying and valuing photographed contents. **I would disqualify the broad photo-to-valued-inventory product; historical-photo reconstruction is only a narrow remaining distinction.**

   **Breaker:** Photos cannot establish hidden possessions, ownership, exact models or continued possession. California also [reduced immediate inventory requirements](https://www.insurance.ca.gov/0400-news/0100-press-releases/2025/release067-2025.cfm) for eligible total losses.

   **48-hour assessment:** Yes: genuine extraction, deduplication, evidence crops and sourced replacement candidates. Completeness and insurer acceptance are shell.

   **Reaction:** A background object in an old birthday video becomes an evidence-linked inventory line.

   **Judge:** “Will insurers accept it?” Honest answer: reviewable documentation, no guaranteed acceptance.

   **Friday kill:** Use uncurated family photos against an owner-verified inventory. Unsupported valuables or repeated double-counting kill it.

7. **News desert agent — real need, established category**

   **Read:** [Hamlet](https://www.myhamlet.com/pricing) is **established commercial** meeting intelligence and alerts. [Citizen Portal](https://citizenportal.ai/help) is **established commercial** location-based government feeds, analysis and alerts. [Locunity](https://www.locunity.com/civic-intelligence) is **live commercial**, scale unverified, monitoring meetings and delivering briefings. **Disqualify the broad monitoring-and-alerting product.** Precise parcel impact is a possible pivot.

   **Breaker:** An address mentioned in discussion does not define the affected area. You need attachments, boundaries and decision status; unpublished meetings remain invisible.

   **48-hour assessment:** Yes for one real meeting, one affected polygon, address intersection and a sourced notification. Regional completeness is shell.

   **Reaction:** A judge’s address intersects an actual proposed change.

   **Judge:** “Why not Hamlet?” Honest answer needs demonstrated geographic impact calculation.

   **Friday kill:** Pick five random addresses and an unselected recent meeting. Generic citywide summaries fail the premise.

8. **Bodycam versus report — commercially disqualified**

   **Read:** [JusticeText](https://www.justicetext.com/) is **established**, explicitly spotting inconsistencies across evidence; [county procurement records](https://www.kanecountyil.gov/MeetingMinutes/7590/25-10-14%20COB%20Minutes.pdf) confirm actual purchase. [FrameCounsel](https://framecounsel.com/) is **live commercial**, adoption unverified, explicitly flagging report/video discrepancies. [Decosa](https://decosa.ai/legal/report-integrity) is **preview-stage**, with contradiction checking tested on synthetic incidents. **JusticeText disqualifies the candidate.**

   **Breaker:** “Not visible” does not mean “didn’t happen.” Transcription errors, camera occlusion and missing angles generate false contradictions.

   **48-hour assessment:** Yes for short clips and claim-by-claim timestamp links. Exhaustive review and legal conclusions are shell. [Official footage exists](https://www.minneapolismn.gov/resident-services/public-safety/police-public-safety/police-reports-and-data-requests/frequently-requested/critical-incidents/sept-2-2026-critical-incident/); a usable matched report remains a separate requirement.

   **Reaction:** Clicking an alleged contradiction plays the decisive moment.

   **Judge:** “Why not JusticeText?” No strong answer as pitched.

   **Friday kill:** Test faithful statements alongside planted contradictions. Confusing absent evidence with contradiction kills it.

9. **Deaf ER — weakest risk-adjusted choice**

   **Read:** [Sign-Speak](https://itsaccessibility.syr.edu/sign-speak-interpreting-service/) is **deployed commercial** bidirectional ASL/speech with an avatar. [Hand Talk](https://www.handtalk.me/en/app/) is **established** signing-avatar output. [MediSign Assist](https://github.com/Sorci3/medisign-assist) is **research** recognizing 20 medical signs in French-Belgian sign language, with acknowledged mobile degradation. **The broad bidirectional translator is disqualified.**

   **Breaker:** Medical meaning includes negation, timing, facial grammar and unfamiliar signers—not isolated hand gestures.

   **48-hour assessment:** Possible for a tiny calibrated vocabulary and reviewed canned avatar responses. Genuine medical interpretation is shell.

   **Reaction:** A short signed exchange becomes audible, then visibly signs back.

   **Judge:** “Who verified the meaning?” Without a fluent Deaf reviewer, no honest adequacy claim.

   **Friday kill:** Have an unfamiliar fluent signer test negatives and contrasts without coaching. Meaning reversals—or inability to obtain review—kill it.
