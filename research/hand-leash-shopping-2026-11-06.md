## Tonight, in person (these need fit and feel)
| Get | Why | Try |
|---|---|---|
| Half-finger lifting or cycling gloves with a Velcro wrist closure, sizes M and L | Stiffer than knit, so anchors don't stretch away from the pull. Replaces the $5.99 knit gloves | Big 5, Dick's, Target, Walmart |
| Braided fishing line, 30-50 lb | Barely stretches, so the hand responds immediately | Walmart, Big 5, Dick's |
| Small snap swivels | Swap lines and gloves quickly between judges | Same fishing aisle |
| Rigid forearm base: archery arm guard, or a forearm brace with a stiff stay | Servos need a base that doesn't shift. The wrist must stay free | Big 5, Dick's, Walmart, CVS |
| 1-inch hook-and-loop strap, heavy mounting tape, small zip ties, foam weatherstrip | Mount and pad the servos | Home Depot, Walmart |
| Small D-rings, key rings, heavy thread and needle | Anchor points on the glove | Michaels, Walmart |
| Cheap multimeter | Confirms 5V wires and whether the board's 5V pin is live | Harbor Freight |
| Dental floss | Tonight's tug test | Any drugstore |
