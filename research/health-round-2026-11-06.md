## Still Here
Problem: a family sits beside someone unresponsive after a brain injury. Bedside commands get no visible reaction, while decisions about care may loom (reasoned).
Demo: a judge lies still with eyes closed. A phone gives spoken commands at random times. Normal video shows nothing; the magnified view moves on cue, and a score shows responses against rest periods. Then a yes/no question. A healthy judge shows the sensing works, not that it works on patients.
Evidence: SeeMe (Aug 2025), 37 coma patients: camera eye responses 30/36 versus 25/36 clinically; 4.1 days earlier (paired, n=24). Mouth 8.3 days earlier (n=7). NEJM 2024: 60/241 (25%) without visible command following responded on fMRI/EEG; convenience sample, median 7.9 months after injury. Yes/no communication not demonstrated by SeeMe. False positives, sedation and specificity are open.
Rivals: Codex verdict KILLED on the science (SeeMe, mindBEAGLE EEG, a CAS patent). Advisor view: Reeve's brief allows prior research; no phone product or hackathon project found, and gallery pass found none. 2026 SeeMe registry NCT07560631 is unread.
Toughest question: "Are you giving families false hope?" Answer direction: random timing and rest periods act as the control, results go to a clinician, never a diagnosis.
Kill test, about 2 hours: phone on a stand in hall light, 20 commands and 20 rests, smallest possible movements, a person watching tries to call them. Software must clearly beat the watcher with few false alarms.
Users: ICU nurses, neurologists, families of survivors; Sac State nursing, UC Davis Medical Center (reasoned leads, nobody contacted).

## Tongue X-Ray
Problem: a child who says "wabbit" sees a speech therapist weekly, then practices at home unable to hear the difference. Ultrasound feedback exists only in clinics (reasoned).
Demo: a judge says "red" and sees their tongue move live inside a 3D head from a microphone, then steers it to a target.
Evidence: 2026 randomized trial, 108 children aged 9-15: visual biofeedback improved /r/ generalization over motor treatment alone. That supports feedback methods, not an inferred tongue. SPARC reports correlation 0.878 on held-out single-dataset speech, 2D points only. Accuracy on a child's actual error is unestablished.
Rivals: CROWDED. Isiko (TreeHacks 2024, mic to 3D vocal tract, says streaming unfinished), LARYNX (HackIllinois 2026, deepfake detection), Hide Your CLB (2026, 2D Mandarin), staRt app (spectrum display, no tongue).
Toughest question: "Is that really my tongue?" Must show accuracy against MRI or ultrasound data.
Users: Sac State speech clinic, 916-278-6989 (older listing), speech therapists on campus.

## Last Muscle
Problem: late ALS or locked-in; even eye control can deteriorate, and EMG switches need hardware.
Demo: someone sits apparently motionless while words appear and their cloned voice speaks.
Rivals: CROWDED. Motif (Oct 2026, webcam facial switch), movo (OxHack first place, Oct 2026), Residual (Oct 2026), Cheshire (Apr 2026, read: phone sensors, wrist or finger gestures, cloned voice). All use visible movement. Google Camera Switch, NeuroNode are shipped.
Left: truly invisible switching; no evidence it works reliably, and unvalidated in ALS. It could become the yes/no channel inside Still Here.
