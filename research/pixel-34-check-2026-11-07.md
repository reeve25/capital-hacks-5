## Findings

1. **Chrome's long-press haptic kills the line hold after about 0.3 s (Measured, 11 of 11 held touches plus 2 e2e holds).**
   - A finger resting on a line gets a hold that starts about 193 ms after touch-down and plays 300-305 ms.
   - Chrome's own long-press feedback then fires about 497 ms after touch-down (`performHapticFeedback(constant=0)`, `Prebaked=HEAVY_CLICK`, usage TOUCH, about 110 ms) and supersedes the hold. The hold's end time is the click's creation time plus 3 ms.
   - After that there is nothing until the lift, or until the 4.5 s re-arm.
   - Felt result: 0.3 s of buzz, a heavy click, then up to about 4 s of silence on the line.
   - `touch-action: none` (already on `#board`) does not stop it.
   - The stage now draws held rings for the full 5 s pattern (PR after #60), so it would show rings while the phone is silent.
   - Moving fingers are not affected: long-press needs the finger to stay still for about 500 ms.
2. **One line fixes findings 1 and 2 and the short-tap gap (Measured, in-memory experiment 14:38:38-14:38:54, listener removed after):** `canvas.addEventListener('touchstart', (e) => e.preventDefault(), { passive: false })` in `feel.html`. With it:
   - 0 long-press clicks.
   - Holds ran to the lift (1489-1512 ms on 1.5 s touches).
   - Touch-down first-buzz delay fell from 192-197 ms to **3-10 ms** (median 4; 7 of 7).
   - 150 ms taps buzzed 145-147 ms instead of nothing.

   Not tested with a real finger, and not tested for side effects. Pointer events and finger reports still arrived; multi-finger pen and 3-finger home were not exercised. Main `5ad8107` doesn't have it. This is Reeve's lane (`feel.html`). **Reasoned, not verified:** the 193 ms looks like Chrome holding the vibrate timer task until the touchstart is handled or the gesture is classified, which is why consuming touchstart removes it.
3. **A call made while the previous on-step is in flight loses its first step (Measured: 2 of 44 accepted stops lost, both 6 ms or less after a fade call; Read in source).**
   - In Blink's `VibrationController`, `DoVibrate` returns early while `is_calling_vibrate_` is set. The old step's `DidVibrate` then erases the *new* pattern's first entry and its pause.
   - A `vibrate(1)` stop is dropped entirely. Run C and the shapes probe each lost one: the last fade pulse played, then nothing. Here the stray pulses were 12-30 ms, so the effect was small.
   - The same race would drop a `[5000]` hold entirely, leaving silence until the 4.5 s re-arm. All 3 fade-to-hold transitions seen today played.
   - Source: `third_party/blink/renderer/modules/vibration/vibration_controller.cc`, Chromium main, read 14:37. The Pixel's Chrome version may differ.
4. **`pixel_e2e.py`'s default `--relay http://localhost:8765` adds about 2 s per connection on this laptop (Measured).** Python `http.client` takes 2039 ms to `localhost` vs 2 ms to `127.0.0.1` (IPv6 first, then fallback); curl takes 210 vs 15 ms. Run A's "tutor -> phone: 2057 ms" is that artifact. Use `--relay http://127.0.0.1:8765`, or change the default at `tools/pixel_e2e.py:706`.
5. **The tool's matcher slack (`match_haptics(slack_ms=10)`) can assign a pulse to the wrong call.** Run A showed 1 "wrong length". Haptic `t` and dumpsys times are both on the phone clock, and an entry can't come before its call, so 1 ms slack is enough. With 1 ms, run A matched with 0 wrong lengths.
6. **Duplicates:** 0 of 112. Nothing failed, so the retry path Codex flagged (P1 #2) was never exercised. The relay restart (14:34:11-14:34:30) came while the phone was idle.
