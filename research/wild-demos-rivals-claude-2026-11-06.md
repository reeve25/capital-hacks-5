Date: 2026-11-06. Event: Capital Hacks 5.0, Sac State, Nov 6-8, 2026.

How to read this:
- "Not found" means not found in these searches. It does not mean nothing exists.
- **Read** = I opened the page. For Devpost that means the project description excerpt (usually the "What it does" section, 700-1800 characters) and the prize line. Non-Devpost pages went through WebFetch, which returns a model summary of the page.
- **Secondary** = search snippet, tagline or search-tool summary only.
- Devpost dates are the page's first timestamp (project started or updated), not the submission date.
- Win status comes from the page's prize line. The search JSON `winner` field is unreliable: it said false for Mirage, Re:collection, CrashDaddy, MatchVision and VITAL, and each of those pages shows a "Winner" line.

**Verdict: KILLED.** The overdose version is a widely covered 2019 paper, and the same phone sonar breathing sensor was rebuilt at least twice in Sep 2026, once at a hackathon. Inaudible phone sonar tracks chest motion with no wearable; about 90% detection in 94 people at a Vancouver supervised injection site and 19 of 20 simulated overdoses; alarm asks the user to respond, then the phone would contact help.
