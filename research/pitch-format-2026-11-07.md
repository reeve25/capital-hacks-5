## What this changes (Reasoned)
- The audience can't feel a vibration through a slide, and GIFs are silent. The only way the room sees the haptics is the stage view: finger, trail and a ring on every real buzz. The stage UI becomes the camera for the invisible part, so it matters more, not less.
- Recorded GIFs only prove "real" if a real hand and phone are in frame. A screen capture of the stage alone looks like an animation.
- Live-demo hardening (judge onboarding, reconnect handling, device chips, presentation mode, reset flows, the iPad network path) no longer pays off. Things only have to survive a recording take, and we can retake.
- The Validation slide needs real numbers: the measured Pixel findings in STATUS.md and the blind shape run, reported in full.
- A PDF deck would freeze every GIF. PowerPoint, Google Slides and Keynote animate GIFs while presenting.
