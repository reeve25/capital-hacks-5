# Winning idea bar: interview spec

Interview Nov 6, 2026, about 15:15-15:35 PT. Reeve and Nathan answered together at the keyboard (their statement; only Reeve's account was typing). Quotes are theirs. "Reasoned" marks my inference, which they have not confirmed.

## The bar in one paragraph
Use AI-assisted engineering to build something two people could not have built before, which fixes a problem that seems impressive to fix: one people assume is permanent, or one whose fix costs a fortune today. Anyone looking at the invention should see at once that it fixes the problem directly and practically. Judges should ask to try it. The thing speaks for itself.

## Auto-reject before any rival check
Chatbots or voice agents. Face gestures or lip reading as the core. Dashboards or reports. AR or VR overlays. Alert or notification apps. Anything needing a score, study or explanation before the benefit shows. Anything that does not stand out, or that would probably not win even if built.

## Rival rule
- **Dead:** a similar project with the same pitch won a prize in about the last 2 years. Read the page to confirm the prize, since winner labels can be participation prizes.
- **Flag and show:** older or unplaced projects, research, and shipped products. They did not choose to kill shipped products, but judges may ask, so each pitch answers "doesn't X do this?"
- Follow CLAUDE.md "Evidence": read the real project, walk galleries, use a different-vendor check.

## What they see
6-8 quick pitches that passed the Codex check, each with: the problem and who has it, the one-step demo, why it is only possible now, rival status, and the buy list. They react, and only liked ideas go deep. Say which way I lean; they decide together.

## Tests for every idea (reasoned, from the answers above)
1. A bystander retells it in one sentence, having seen only the object and the moment.
2. A judge would walk up and ask for a turn.
3. A two-person team without today's AI could not plausibly have built it this weekend.
4. A fast, cheap kill test can answer "does the core trick work" well before Saturday noon.
5. Parts can be bought under $200 by Saturday morning with no soldering.
6. CLAUDE.md's five questions: one sentence; "that's real"; only possible now; a life visibly changes; survives the toughest question.
