How to use

Explore
Slide one finger across the screen. Lines buzz under your finger, and places say their names.
Hear it again
Double tap with two fingers to hear your tutor's instruction again.
Leave the drawing
Press Back to return to this screen.

Development and validation

No buzz? Turn off Silent and Do Not Disturb, then check that vibration is on in Android settings.
