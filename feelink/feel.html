How to use

Explore by touch
Slide one finger across the screen. Lines buzz under your finger.
Find labels
A label gives three long buzzes and says its name. Your start point says “Start” first.
Find the line again
Off a line, light taps speed up as you get closer.
Leave the drawing
Press Back to return to this screen.

Development and validation

No buzz? Turn off Silent and Do Not Disturb, then check that vibration is on in Android settings.
